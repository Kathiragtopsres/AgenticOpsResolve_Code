# ONT-X200 Troubleshooting Guide

> SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT created by TCS for the OpsResolve hackathon demo. Not a real OEM document.

Optical receive power below -25 dBm with high FEC errors and repeated resets indicates a failing ONT or dirty connector. Safe remote action: `ONT_REBOOT`. If errors persist after reboot, replace ONT (customer-replaceable on business plans).
